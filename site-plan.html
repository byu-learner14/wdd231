Hawai‘i Surf & Ocean Safety

Individual Website Project — Site Plan

1. Site Name

Hawai‘i Surf & Ocean Safety

Reason: The name is clear, descriptive, and immediately tells visitors the website focuses on surfing and ocean safety in Hawai‘i. It supports strong branding and SEO.

2. Site Purpose

This website provides valuable information and safety education for surfers and ocean-goers in Hawai‘i. It offers details about popular surf spots, real-time ocean conditions, and essential safety guidelines to help users enjoy the ocean responsibly and reduce risks.

3. Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: A first-time visitor to Maui wants to know safe beginner surf spots and what precautions to take. They use the site to research locations and safety tips before their trip.
  • Scenario 2: A local resident checks current ocean conditions and reviews rip current safety information before going surfing in the morning.

4. Color Scheme

Primary Color: Deep Ocean Blue (#003087) — Used for headers, navigation background, and main accents.

Secondary Color: Turquoise (#00B2A9) — Used for buttons, links, highlights, and hover effects.

Supporting Colors: Sandy Beige (#EDE0C8) for subtle backgrounds, White for content areas, and Dark Gray (#222222) for text.

5. Typography

  • Headings: System sans-serif (Poppins-style) — Bold and modern. Used for all h1, h2, h3.
  • Body Text: System UI / Segoe UI — Clean and highly readable. Used for all paragraphs and lists.
